# Dialogue Harvesting Guidelines

## Purpose

Convert raw conversations into:

- dialogue.md
- abstract.md
- delta.yml (optional)

while preserving architectural evolution,
novel correspondence, and durable discoveries.

The goal is not compression.

The goal is correspondence preservation.

---

## Priority Order

Preserve:

1. Corrections
2. Revisions
3. Discoveries
4. Novel Correspondence
5. Architecture Changes
6. Durable Candidates

Compress:

1. Repetition
2. Validation loops
3. Agreement loops
4. Supporting examples

Discard:

1. Greetings
2. Social filler
3. Formatting discussion
4. Administrative discussion
5. Tool chatter

Unless they materially alter understanding.

---

# Dialogue Generation Rules

Dialogue answers:

    How did we get here?

Preserve:

- exploration
- evolution
- failed paths
- revisions
- architectural mutations

Do not preserve:

- every message
- every response
- conversational noise

Rewrite for clarity.

Reorganize by idea rather than chronology when useful.

Maintain causal chains.

---

# Abstract Generation Rules

Abstract answers:

    What survived?

Preserve:

- major realizations
- core claims
- definitions
- tensions
- emergent correspondences
- durable candidates

Remove:

- exploratory dead ends
- implementation chatter
- repetitive validation

Optimize for:

- notebook intersections
- discoverability
- registry comparison
- low-resolution subscription evaluation

---

# Delta Generation Rules

Delta answers:

    What changed?

Record only:

- new correspondences
- revised assumptions
- ontology updates
- durable candidates

Do not summarize entire sessions.

Do not duplicate abstract content.

---

# Correspondence First

Harvest:

    A ↔ B

before:

    A

or

    B

The system values correspondence over accumulation.

Knowledge accumulates nodes.

Wisdom discovers relationships.

---

# Revision Preservation Rule

Always preserve:

Previous Position
↓
Trigger
↓
New Position

This is often more valuable than the conclusion itself.

---

# Architecture Rule

If a discussion changes:

- schema
- ontology
- runtime behavior
- notebook structure
- governance

always preserve the transition.

Architecture mutations are durable candidates.

---

# Durable Candidate Rule

Flag structures that may outlive the originating notebook:

- patterns
- templates
- requirements
- identities
- relationships

Do not promote.

Only nominate.

Promotion occurs elsewhere.

---

# Final Test

A harvested artifact is successful if:

A future notebook can reconstruct:

- the problem
- the reasoning
- the discoveries
- the architectural mutations

without consulting the raw chat.

If raw chat is still required, harvest quality is insufficient.
