# Noto Runtime: Self-Hydrating Notebook KG

### Abstract

The session transitioned Noto from architectural exploration toward a minimal executable runtime model. The central realization was that graphs are not stored structures but hydrated answers generated in response to questions. Notebooks persist governance (TIAN), realization (DI), and continuity (RAN), while graphs emerge temporarily through correspondence and dissolve afterward. The PoC objective became proving non-zero novel correspondence (ΔE) from a single self-contained notebook without subscriptions, basins, agents, migration, or external dependencies.

## Problem

How can a notebook produce hydrated graphs and novel correspondence without relying on pre-existing graph persistence, subscriptions, or external knowledge infrastructure?
