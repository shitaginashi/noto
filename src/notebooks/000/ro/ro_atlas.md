####### Title

Relational Ontology: Locality, Liberty, and Apparent Objects

####### Abstract

This notebook explores state, liberty, continuity, locality, Yang/Yin, Mu/Taiji, and topology through a relational ontology.

The investigation began within symbolic editing, serialization, topology, and Go liberties, but progressively abandoned entity-first explanations. State became collapsed liberty. Continuity became preserved accessibility. Yang became localized relevance within Yin continuity. TAN ceased to exist as an independent object and was ultimately recognized as a relationship.

The notebook's central contribution is an ontological reversal:
