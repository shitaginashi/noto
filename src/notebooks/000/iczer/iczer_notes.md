## Humanity, Synchronization, and the Veil

### Context

The discussion began as an analysis of *Fight! Iczer-1* and its relationship to cosmic horror.

Initial focus centered on:

- Nagisa as humanity's representative
- assimilation and body horror
- the Cthulwulf as an external threat
- themes of sacrifice and preservation
- empathy as a force stronger than violence

As the investigation continued, the anime became increasingly incidental.

What remained was an inquiry into:

- humanity
- civilization
- empathy
- sanity
- culture
- shared reality

The notebook is ultimately not about *Iczer-1*.

It is about the ontology that *Iczer-1* exposed.

---

### Starting Position

Several assumptions appeared reasonable:

- Nagisa = Humanity
- Big Gold = Evil
- Cthulwulf = Cosmic Horror
- Humanity = Species
- Civilization = Society
- Veil = Barrier

All survived partially.

None survived unchanged.
