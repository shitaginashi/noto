# Enlightenment as Pattern, Antipattern, and Mu
 
## Context
 
The discussion began with a deceptively simple question:
 
Can enlightenment be defined in a secular, objective, empirical way?
 
Rather than treating enlightenment as mystical attainment, the investigation attempted to model enlightenment as a cognitive process grounded in perception, updating, pattern recognition, and relationship formation.
 
The conversation gradually shifted through:
 
- cognition
- graph theory
- systems theory
- topology
- Taoism
- Buddhism
- information theory
 
The resulting notebook is not fundamentally about spirituality.
 
It is about the relationship between knowledge, wisdom, relevance, and possibility.
 
---
