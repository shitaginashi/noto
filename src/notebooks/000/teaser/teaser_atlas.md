###### Title

Narrative Compression Architecture

####### Abstract

A practical teaser-trailer exercise using orphan footage, calibration clips, and text cards evolved into an investigation of narrative compression, context generation, and story construction.

The notebook progressively separated narrative from story, defining narrative as transmitted structure and story as audience construction. Narrative compression and atmosphere emerged as complementary mechanisms for producing context. Escalation expanded perceived narrative scope without requiring additional exposition. Mystery was ultimately redefined as guided incompleteness rather than concealment.

The resulting framework models teasers not as miniature stories but as systems that transmit compressed narrative conditions from which audiences construct stories and derive meaning.
